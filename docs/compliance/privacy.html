隐私政策

最近更新：2026 年 8 月 31 日 · 生效日期：2026 年 8 月 31 日

本隐私政策说明 Skiller（以下称"本应用""我们"）在你使用本 iOS 应用时如何收集、使用、存储、共享和保护你的个人信息。我们坚持最小必要原则，并对每一项数据处理保持透明。请在使用本应用前完整阅读本政策。

1. 个人信息处理者与联系方式

本应用由个人开发者 duskecho 开发与运营。如对本政策或你的个人信息有任何疑问、投诉或权利请求，可通过电子邮件联系我们：handwanly@gmail.com。本应用的工业和信息化部备案编号在 App 内"我的"页面底部展示。

2. 我们如何征得你的同意

你首次启动本应用时，会先看到隐私同意提示。在你明确点击"同意并继续"之前，本应用不会启动账号、技能目录、收藏同步或广告服务，也不会主动发起这些服务的网络请求。如果你主动点击隐私政策或使用条款链接，系统浏览器会按你的操作加载相应网页。仅在你同意后，下述功能与数据处理才会启用。你可以选择不同意，此时本应用不提供服务。广告法规要求提供额外选择时，Google User Messaging Platform 会另行展示广告隐私选项。

5. 关于广告

本应用将 Google Mobile Ads SDK 配置为非个性化广告，并关闭发布商第一方标识符。非个性化广告不根据你的历史行为选择广告，但 Google 说明这类广告仍可能使用 Cookie 或移动广告标识符进行频次控制和汇总报告。我们不请求 App Tracking Transparency 权限，因此本应用不能访问 IDFA；这不代表广告 SDK 完全不处理设备或网络信息。

你同意本政策后，本应用会先通过 Google User Messaging Platform 更新广告同意状态并展示适用的隐私选择。只有该平台确认可以请求广告时，应用才启动广告 SDK 和加载广告。适用地区要求长期提供选择入口时，"我的"页面会显示"广告隐私选项"。

6. 个人信息的跨境传输（重要）

本应用的账号鉴权、技能数据库、账号收藏同步和举报存储由 Supabase, Inc. 提供，相关基础设施位于中华人民共和国境外。登录后，你的账号信息、用户 ID、收藏的技能 ID 和同步时间戳会被传输至境外并存储；你主动提交举报时，举报内容也会传输至境外并存储。

境外接收方为 Supabase, Inc.，所在国家为美国，接收目的为提供账号鉴权、数据存储、多设备收藏同步和举报处理。当你点击"同意并继续"时，即表示你已知悉并单独同意上述个人信息跨境传输。我们仅传输实现功能所必需的信息。

7. 个人信息的保存期限

  • 账号与账号收藏：在你的账号存续期间保留。账号删除成功后，在线账号记录及其收藏记录会一并删除。
  • 本地账号缓存：账号删除成功后从当前设备清除。
  • 游客收藏与最近浏览：保存在你的设备上，直至你删除本应用。
  • 举报：在处理举报和保留必要审核记录期间保存。你删除账号后，举报中的账号用户 ID 会与账号解除关联；如需删除举报内容，可通过下方邮箱联系我们。

8. 你的权利

在适用法律范围内，你有权访问、更正、复制、删除我们持有的你的个人信息，撤回同意，以及注销账号：

  1. 注销账号：打开 App → "我的" → 点击头像卡片下方的"删除账号" → 确认。该操作将永久删除你的账号凭据、服务器账号收藏及当前设备上的账号收藏缓存，不可撤销。独立的游客收藏和最近浏览记录仍保留在设备上，直至你删除本应用；既有举报会解除与你账号用户 ID 的关联。
  2. 撤回同意：你可注销账号并停止使用、卸载本应用以撤回同意；如需删除仍保留在服务器上的数据，可通过下方邮箱联系我们。
  3. 访问、更正、复制：通过下方邮箱向我们提出请求，我们将在合理期限内响应。

9. 未成年人保护

本应用不面向 14 周岁以下儿童，我们不会在知情情况下收集儿童个人信息。如你为儿童监护人且发现儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请通过下方邮箱联系我们，我们将及时删除。若你为未满 18 周岁的未成年人，请在监护人同意并指导下使用本应用。

10. 信息安全

我们通过传输加密（HTTPS）等技术与管理措施保护你的个人信息安全。但请注意，任何网络传输与存储均无法保证绝对安全。

11. 本政策的更新

我们可能不时更新本政策。重大变更将通过更新本页顶部的"最近更新"日期体现；涉及你权益的重大变更，我们将以应用内提示等显著方式告知。变更后你继续使用本应用即视为接受更新后的政策。

12. 投诉与联系

关于本政策或你个人信息的任何问题、投诉，请发送邮件至 handwanly@gmail.com。如你认为我们的个人信息处理行为损害了你的合法权益，且与我们协商无法解决，你也可以向网信、电信等主管部门投诉举报。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026 · Effective: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Skiller ("we", "the app") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your information when you use our iOS application. We follow a data minimization principle and aim to be transparent about every processing activity. Please read this policy in full before using the app.

This app is developed and operated by an individual developer, duskecho. For any questions, complaints, or rights requests regarding this policy or your personal information, contact us at handwanly@gmail.com. The app's MIIT (ICP) filing number is displayed at the bottom of the in-app Profile page.

2. How We Obtain Your Consent

On first launch, you are shown a privacy consent screen. Until you explicitly tap "Agree and Continue", the app does not start account, Skills directory, favorites sync, or advertising services, and does not proactively make network requests to those services. If you choose a Privacy Policy or Terms link, the system browser loads that page at your direction. The features and processing below are enabled only after you agree. If you decline, the app does not provide its service. Where advertising rules require additional choices, Google User Messaging Platform presents a separate advertising privacy form.

5. Advertising

We configure the Google Mobile Ads SDK for non-personalized ads and disable the publisher first-party identifier. Non-personalized ads are not selected from your past behavior, but Google states that they may still use cookies or mobile advertising identifiers for frequency capping and aggregate reporting. We do not request App Tracking Transparency permission, so the app cannot access the IDFA. This does not mean the advertising SDK processes no device or network information.

After you accept this policy, the app first asks Google User Messaging Platform to update advertising consent status and present any privacy choices that apply. The app starts the advertising SDK and loads ads only when that platform reports that ads may be requested. If your region requires an ongoing choice, an "Ad Privacy Options" entry appears on the Profile page.

6. Cross-Border Data Transfer (Important)

Authentication, the Skills database, account-favorites sync, and report storage are provided by Supabase, Inc. using infrastructure outside the People's Republic of China. After sign-in, your account information, user ID, favorited Skill IDs, and sync timestamps are transferred overseas and stored there. Report content is also transferred overseas and stored when you submit it. The overseas recipient is Supabase, Inc. in the United States, for authentication, data storage, multi-device favorites sync, and report handling. By tapping "Agree and Continue", you acknowledge and separately consent to this cross-border transfer. We transfer only information needed to provide these features.

7. Data Retention

  • Account and account favorites: kept while your account exists. When account deletion succeeds, the online account record and its favorites are deleted together.
  • Local account cache: cleared from the current device after account deletion succeeds.
  • Guest favorites and recent viewing history: stay on your device until you delete the app.
  • Reports: kept while needed to process the report and maintain necessary review records. Account deletion unlinks your account user ID from existing reports; email us if you want report content deleted.

8. Your Rights

  1. Delete account: Profile → "Delete account" below your profile card → confirm. Permanently deletes your credentials, server-side account favorites, and the current device's account-favorites cache; this cannot be undone. Separate guest favorites and recent viewing history stay on your device until you delete the app. Existing reports are unlinked from your account user ID.
  2. Withdraw consent: delete your account, stop using, and uninstall the app; email us to remove any server-side data that remains.
  3. Access / correct / export: email us; we respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Guardians who believe a child has provided us data should contact us for prompt deletion. Minors under 18 should use the app under guardian consent and guidance.

10. Security

We protect your information with measures such as encrypted transmission (HTTPS). However, no method of transmission or storage is absolutely secure.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes are reflected by the "Last updated" date and, where they affect your rights, communicated prominently in-app. Continued use after changes means acceptance.

12. Complaints & Contact

Questions or complaints: handwanly@gmail.com. If you believe our processing harms your lawful rights and cannot be resolved with us, you may also complain to the competent cyberspace/telecom authorities.
